# AeroAssist Airline Policy: Seat Selection & Cabin Upgrades

## Document Overview
- **Document ID:** DOC-AIR-006
- **Category:** Seat Selection
- **Target Service:** Commercial Ancillary Revenue & Inflight Experience

---

## 1. Seat Selection Rules & Timing
- **Standard Seats:**
  - Free selection opens during online check-in (24 hours prior to departure) for all fare classes.
  - Advance selection prior to 24 hours available for a nominal fee ($15 - $35 USD) on Basic Economy fares; complimentary for Flex Economy, Business, and Platinum tier members.
- **Extra Legroom & Exit Row Seats:**
  - Provides an additional 3 to 5 inches of pitch.
  - Available for purchase at any time up to 1 hour before departure ($45 - $95 USD depending on stage length).

---

## 2. Emergency Exit Row Safety Criteria
Federal and International aviation regulations (FAA 14 CFR § 121.585 / EASA CAT.OP.MPA.165) mandate that passengers occupying exit row seats must satisfy mandatory physical and cognitive requirements:
1. **Age Requirement:** Must be at least **15 years of age** on the day of departure.
2. **Physical Capability:** Must possess sufficient mobility, strength, and dexterity in both arms, hands, and legs to quickly open heavy emergency exit doors (weighing up to 45 lbs / 20 kg).
3. **Communication:** Must understand oral and written English or local language emergency commands from flight attendants and convey safety instructions to other passengers.
4. **Restrictions:** Passengers traveling with infants, service animals, seatbelt extenders, pregnant beyond 28 weeks, or having a visual/hearing impairment cannot occupy emergency exit rows. Inflight crew has legal authority to reassign seats without refund if safety criteria are not met.

---

## 3. Cabin Upgrades
- **Upgrade Channels:**
  - Online self-service bid upgrade up to 12 hours before departure.
  - Airport counter upgrade on space-available basis at fixed pricing.
- **Baggage Allowance on Upgrades:** Passengers who purchase an upgrade to Business Class inherit the full Business Class baggage allowance (2 bags up to 32 kg each) and priority boarding privileges.
