# AeroAssist Airline Policy: Flight Delays & Disruption Assistance

## Document Overview
- **Document ID:** DOC-AIR-005
- **Category:** Flight Delay
- **Target Service:** Integrated Operations Control Center (IOCC) & Station Management
- **Mandate:** Passenger Duty of Care & Disruption Relief Guidelines

---

## 1. Delay Tiers & Airline Duty of Care

AeroAssist provides tiered assistance whenever a flight departure is delayed:

### Tier 1: Delay Between 2 and 4 Hours
- Complimentary digital or paper **Meal & Refreshment Voucher** ($20 USD domestic / $35 USD international per passenger).
- Two complimentary telephone calls, telex, fax messages, or emails (free airport terminal Wi-Fi access voucher provided).

### Tier 2: Delay Exceeding 4 Hours
- Additional food and beverage provisions proportional to waiting time.
- Priority customer service desks opened at boarding gate to answer passenger inquiries.
- Option to voluntarily rebook on an alternate AeroAssist flight within 14 days without change fee or fare difference.

### Tier 3: Overnight Delay or Delay Exceeding 6 Hours
- **Complimentary Hotel Accommodation:** AeroAssist provides 3-star or 4-star contracted hotel accommodation for all non-local passengers.
- **Ground Transportation:** Free shuttle bus or taxi voucher between airport terminal and assigned hotel.
- **Full Refund Right:** If delay exceeds 5 hours and passenger chooses not to travel, passenger may request a 100% full refund of the ticket plus return flight to first point of departure at the earliest opportunity.

---

## 2. Missed Connecting Flights Due to Inbound Delay
When multi-leg itineraries booked under a single ticket (single PNR) experience a missed connection due to late arrival of the feeder flight:
1. **Automatic Protected Rebooking:** The airline reservations system automatically rebooks the passenger on the next available flight to the final destination.
2. **Boarding Pass Collection:** Passenger receives updated boarding pass via SMS, email, or at the Transit Services counter.
3. **Transit Hotel Accommodation:** Provided if connecting departure occurs on the following calendar day with a layover exceeding 6 hours.
4. **Baggage Transfer:** Checked luggage is automatically retagged and transferred to the rescheduled connecting flight.
