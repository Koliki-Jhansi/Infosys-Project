# AeroAssist Airline Policy: Special Assistance & Accessibility Services

## Document Overview
- **Document ID:** DOC-AIR-007
- **Category:** Special Assistance
- **Target Service:** Airport Special Services & Inflight Accessibility
- **Regulation Compliance:** US 14 CFR Part 382 / EU Regulation 1107/2006

---

## 1. Wheelchair & Mobility Assistance Codes
To ensure seamless airport transfers from curb to aircraft cabin, passengers requiring mobility assistance should request service at least **48 hours prior to scheduled departure**:

- **WCHR (Ramp):** Passenger can ascend and descend aircraft steps and walk to seat, but requires wheelchair assistance for long distances between check-in counter and boarding gate.
- **WCHS (Steps):** Passenger cannot ascend or descend stairs, but is able to walk inside the aircraft cabin to their designated seat. Requires wheelchair to aircraft door and high-lift or ambulift vehicle.
- **WCHC (Cabin Seat):** Passenger is completely immobile and requires wheelchair assistance to aircraft door and onboard aisle chair transfer directly into aircraft passenger seat.

---

## 2. Personal Wheelchairs & Battery Equipment
- **Manual Wheelchairs:** Carried in aircraft cargo hold free of charge, in addition to standard baggage allowance. Stowed with high priority.
- **Electric / Motorized Wheelchairs:**
  - Lithium-ion batteries must be disconnected, terminals insulated, and certified under UN Manual of Tests Part III Subsection 38.3.
  - Non-spillable wet or dry gel batteries may remain installed if securely attached to the wheelchair frame.
  - Passenger must provide battery specifications at airport check-in.

---

## 3. Unaccompanied Minors (UMNR)
- **Age Eligibility:** Mandatory for children aged **5 to 11 years** traveling without a parent, legal guardian, or adult aged 18+. Optional for ages 12 to 14.
- **Procedures:** Dedicated AeroAssist ground escort stays with child through security, immigration, boarding, and hands over to cabin crew supervisor.
- **Collection:** Authorized recipient at destination airport must present government photo ID matching the legal UMNR declaration form signed at origin.
- **Service Fee:** $100 USD domestic / $150 USD international per unaccompanied child each way.

---

## 4. Medical Clearance (MEDIF Form)
Passengers with recent surgery (within 10 days), cardiac conditions, communicable diseases, or requiring supplemental medical oxygen during flight must submit a completed **Medical Information Form (MEDIF)** signed by an attending physician at least 72 hours before flight departure.
