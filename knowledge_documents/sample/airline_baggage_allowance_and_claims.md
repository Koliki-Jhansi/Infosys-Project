# AeroAssist Airline Policy: Baggage Allowances, Lost & Delayed Claims

## Document Overview
- **Document ID:** DOC-AIR-003
- **Category:** Baggage
- **Target Service:** Ground Operations & Central Baggage Resolution
- **Governing Treaty:** Montreal Convention 1999 (SDR Liability Limits)

---

## 1. Standard Baggage Allowances

### Carry-On Baggage (Cabin):
- **1 Personal Item:** Laptop bag, purse, or backpack (max $40 \times 30 \times 15$ cm), must fit under seat in front.
- **1 Standard Cabin Bag:** Max $55 \times 40 \times 23$ cm (including wheels and handles), max weight 8 kg (17.6 lbs).
- Premium & Business cabins: 2 standard cabin bags permitted.

### Checked Baggage (Hold):
- **Economy Standard:** 1 bag up to 23 kg (50 lbs), maximum dimensions 158 cm (length + width + height).
- **Flex Economy:** 2 bags up to 23 kg each.
- **Business Class:** 2 bags up to 32 kg (70 lbs) each.
- **Overweight / Oversize Fee:** $75 USD for 24-32 kg; items exceeding 32 kg must be shipped via air cargo.

---

## 2. Delayed Baggage Claim Procedure
When a passenger arrives at their destination airport and their checked baggage is not delivered to the baggage carousel:
1. **Property Irregularity Report (PIR):** Passenger must immediately report to the AeroAssist Baggage Service Desk before exiting customs to file a PIR report. The 10-character PIR reference (e.g. `JFKAA12345`) is linked to global WorldTracer.
2. **Emergency Interim Expense Allowance:**
   - For bags delayed beyond 24 hours away from the passenger's home city:
   - $75 USD per day (up to $225 USD total) for essential toiletries and emergency clothing.
   - Receipts must be retained and submitted through the online claims portal within 21 days of bag delivery.
3. **Baggage Delivery:** As soon as the bag is located, AeroAssist arranges complimentary courier delivery directly to the passenger's hotel or residence.

---

## 3. Damaged Baggage Claims
- **Reporting Deadline:** Must be reported in writing within **7 calendar days** of receipt of baggage.
- Normal wear and tear (minor scuffs, scratches, dirty fabric, broken zipper pulls, or damage resulting from overpacking) is not compensable under airline carriage terms.
- Structural damage (cracked hard shells, torn wheels, destroyed handles) will be repaired, replaced, or reimbursed at depreciated value up to Montreal Convention limits (approx. 1,288 SDRs / ~$1,700 USD).
