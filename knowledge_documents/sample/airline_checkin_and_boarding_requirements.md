# AeroAssist Airline Policy: Check-in & Boarding Requirements

## Document Overview
- **Document ID:** DOC-AIR-004
- **Category:** Check-in and Boarding
- **Target Service:** Airport Terminal Operations & Passenger Gate Control
- **Applies To:** All domestic and international passenger departures

---

## 1. Check-In Windows & Channels

1. **Online / Mobile Check-In:**
   - Opens: **24 hours** prior to scheduled flight departure.
   - Closes: **60 minutes** prior to international departure / **45 minutes** for domestic flights.
   - Mobile boarding passes with 2D barcode (PKPASS format) accepted at all biometric and standard e-gates.
2. **Airport Counter & Kiosk Check-In:**
   - Opens: 3 hours prior to scheduled departure for international flights / 2 hours for domestic.
   - Closes strictly: **60 minutes** prior to international departure / **45 minutes** for domestic flights.
   - Passengers traveling with check-in luggage must complete baggage drop before counter closure.

---

## 2. Boarding Gate Timelines & Final Call
Airlines enforce strict on-time departure performance (D0):
- **Boarding Gate Opens:** 45 minutes prior to departure for wide-body aircraft; 35 minutes for narrow-body aircraft.
- **Boarding Order:**
  - Pre-boarding: Families with infants, unaccompanied minors, passengers requiring wheelchair assistance.
  - Group 1: First Class & Executive Platinum frequent flyers.
  - Group 2: Business Class & Gold frequent flyers.
  - Groups 3 to 5: Economy passengers boarded by row zones (window to aisle).
- **Gate Closure Cutoff:** Strictly **15 minutes** before departure time. After gate closure, passenger offloading is triggered for aircraft weight and balance compliance.

---

## 3. Travel Documentation & Visa Clearance
- **Passport Validity:** International passengers must possess a passport valid for at least 6 months beyond the date of intended return, unless bilateral treaty exemptions apply.
- **Visa Requirements:** Verification performed via IATA Timatic system at check-in. Passengers without confirmed entry visas or transit visas will be denied boarding without carrier liability.
