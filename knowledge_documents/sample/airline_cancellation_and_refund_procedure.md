# AeroAssist Airline Policy: Cancellation & Refund Procedures

## Document Overview
- **Document ID:** DOC-AIR-002
- **Category:** Cancellation and Refund
- **Target Service:** Passenger Revenue Accounting & Claims
- **Applies To:** Voluntary & Involuntary flight cancellations

---

## 1. Voluntary Passenger Cancellation Tiers

Refund eligibility for voluntary cancellation depends on fare brand purchased:

| Fare Category | Cancellation Deadline | Cancellation Fee | Refund Method |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Basic Economy** | Any time prior to departure | Non-refundable | Airport taxes and government security fees only |
| **Standard Economy** | Up to 2 hours before departure | $100 Domestic / $200 International | Residual fare issued as 1-year Flight Credit |
| **Flex Economy** | Up to 1 hour before departure | Free ($0) | 100% refund to original payment card or flight credit |
| **Business / First** | Up to 1 hour before departure | Free ($0) | 100% refund to original payment card |

- **No-Show Policy:** Passengers who fail to cancel at least 60 minutes prior to scheduled departure forfeit ticket value, excluding uncollected government departure taxes.

---

## 2. Involuntary Cancellation (Airline Initiated)

When AeroAssist cancels a flight due to technical malfunction, crew unavailability, air traffic control restrictions, or severe weather:

1. **Immediate Alternate Rebooking:** Complimentary rebooking on the next available AeroAssist flight or partner carrier in the same or higher cabin class.
2. **Full Cash Refund Option:** If the passenger declines the offered alternative, they are entitled to an immediate 100% cash refund of all unused flight segments to the original payment method, regardless of original fare rules.
3. **Statutory Compensation:**
   - Under EC 261/2004 or equivalent regional passenger protection laws:
     - Flights under 1,500 km: €250 compensation.
     - Flights between 1,500 km and 3,500 km: €400 compensation.
     - Flights over 3,500 km: €600 compensation.
   - *Exception:* Extraordinary weather conditions (severe typhoons, volcanic ash, airport closure) exempt cash compensation but maintain mandatory Duty of Care (hotel and meals).

---

## 3. Medical & Bereavement Exceptions
Passengers unable to travel due to sudden serious illness, hospitalization, or death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling):
- Full refund or fee-free rescheduling within 6 months.
- **Required Documentation:** Official signed hospital admission certificate or death certificate submitted within 14 days of original travel date.
- Support agents must apply waiver code `MED-BEREAVE-100`.
