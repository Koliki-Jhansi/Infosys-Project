# AeroAssist Airline Policy: Booking & Reservation Management

## Document Overview
- **Document ID:** DOC-AIR-001
- **Category:** Booking and Reservation
- **Target Service:** Passenger Ticketing & PNR Servicing
- **Applies To:** All commercial scheduled domestic and international flights

---

## 1. 24-Hour Flexible Booking Rule
Passengers who book directly through our official portal or authorized airline support desks are entitled to cancel or modify their reservation within **24 hours of booking** with zero penalty fees, provided the reservation was made at least 7 days prior to scheduled flight departure.

- **Full Refund:** 100% refund of the original fare and airline carrier charges to the original form of payment.
- **Processing Window:** Automated refunds for 24-hour rule requests process within 3 to 5 business days for credit/debit cards.
- **Third-Party Bookings:** Bookings made through Online Travel Agencies (OTAs) such as Expedia or Skyscanner must be altered through the issuing agency unless involuntary disruption has occurred.

---

## 2. Name Correction Policy
Airline security and immigration regulations prohibit the transfer of tickets to another individual. However, minor typographical errors may be corrected without reissuing full fares:
1. **Minor Typos (Up to 3 Characters):** Free correction permitted for spelling mistakes (e.g., "Jonh" to "John") upon presentation of government photo ID or passport copy.
2. **Legal Name Changes:** Permitted upon presentation of legal documentation (marriage certificate, deed poll, court decree). Requires ticket reissue with waiver code `NC-LEGAL-WAIVE`.
3. **First/Last Name Inversion:** Corrections permitted at zero fee if the full legal identity matches the passport.
4. **Complete Passenger Substitution:** Strictly prohibited under ICAO Annex 9 aviation security mandates.

---

## 3. Flight Itinerary Modifications & Fare Differences
Passengers holding Standard Economy, Flex Economy, Business, or First Class tickets may alter flight dates, times, or routes:
- **Change Fees:**
  - *Standard Economy:* $75 USD domestic change fee / $150 USD international change fee plus any applicable fare difference.
  - *Flex Economy & Premium Cabins:* Change fee waived; passenger pays only the fare difference if the new flight segment operates at a higher fare tier.
- **Lower Fare Policy:** If the newly selected flight is priced lower than the original ticket, the residual balance is non-refundable but issued as a transferable 12-month Future Flight Travel Voucher.

---

## 4. Infant & Child Ticketing
- **Infants on Lap (Under 2 Years):** Travel for 10% of adult base fare on international flights; free of charge on domestic routes. Must be added to the adult PNR prior to check-in.
- **Infants in Car Seat:** Requires purchase of an individual child fare ticket with a dedicated aircraft seat. Car seat must bear FAA/EASA aviation safety approval labels.
- **Child Fares (Ages 2 to 11):** Billed at 75% of applicable adult base fare.
